FAQs

Q: What is this weather app?

A: It is a web application that provides weather information and forecasts for supported locations.

Q: Do I need an account?

A: Yes, create an account and log in to access the weather dashboard.

Q: Can I check the weather in different cities?

A: Yes, if the city is supported by the weather service integrated into the application.

Q: Is the app free?

A: Yes, only Identification and Authentication required.

Q: Where does the weather information come from?

About SkyCast

SkyCast is a weather information platform designed to help users explore current weather conditions and forecasts for different loactions. Our goal is to make weather information easy to understand, whether you are planning your daily activities, preparing for a trip, or checking the weather in another city, SkyCast heips you stay informed about the conditions that may affect your plans.

Mission

Our weather platform is designed to help users explore current weather conditions forecasts for supported locations through a simple and user-friendly interface.

Vision

To create a reliable and accessible weather platform that helps people to make more informed decisions about their day.

Values

Accessibility, reliability, simplicity and innovation.
